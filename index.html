Future of Information Lab

The Future of Information Lab is a research group in the School of Information Sciences at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, led by Dr. Marianne Aubin Le Quéré. We conduct mixed-method research in Human-Computer Interaction, Computational Social Science, and Communication.

Research directions

We aim to help people sustain an intentional, trustworthy, and constructive relationship with their online information ecosystems in the generative AI age. Our work currently follows three broad directions.

  1. Agency over information

    Feeds are built to maximize engagement, and many people feel they have lost control of what they see. We design tools, like personalized and agentic feeds, that help people shape their information environments with intent and look after their wellbeing.

    news and mental health · social media and wellbeing · personalized and agentic feeds

    More on this direction →

  2. Trust in AI-mediated information

    AI increasingly shapes how people judge the credibility of civic information. We study how to calibrate trust in AI-generated content, and how journalists, creators and moderators can show the work behind what they publish.

    credibility, trust, and provenance of AI content · generative AI search · AI tools for information workers

    More on this direction →

  3. Attention and informedness

    What people see online shapes how they understand the world, yet that exposure is often siloed or opaque. We measure what civic information people actually encounter, in what contexts, and how they make sense of it.

    news knowledge index · attention to online news · local news availability
